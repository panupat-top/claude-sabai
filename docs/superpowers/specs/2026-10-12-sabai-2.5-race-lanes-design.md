# sabai 2.5.0 — race lanes: footprints and slim lanes — design

Date: 2026-10-12 · Mod: `sabai` · Backlog: R1 · Status: approved in conversation (preview `r1/race.html` in the session scratchpad), awaiting spec review

## Goal

A glance at the race box should tell how far each member has come and which lanes are at work. Today:
1. a lane with no task list or step reports (most of them) holds its runner at the middle, so it looks as if nothing moves;
2. the tiles start at the left edge whatever the runner's place, and a moving lane repeats them to fill its scroll, so the history cannot be read;
3. every lane is 42 px, so Main waiting on agents, or an agent that has finished, takes as much room as one at work, and the band grows with it.

Out of scope: the latest call's name on the step line, per-lane `✓`/`✗` counts, a legend for the tile colours. The box stays 300 px wide and desktop-only.

## 1. Footprints and the runner (idea A)

**The runner's place.**
- With progress known (the task list, the agent's step reports), the runner stands at it, as today.
- Without it, the runner stands at `paceOf(count)`, where `count` is the lane's finished calls:

  ```
  paceOf(n) = max(0.10, min(0.85, 1 − 0.5^(n / 8)))
  ```

  0–1 calls → 10%, 8 calls → 50%, 16 → 75%, from about 22 calls → 85%. The 85% ceiling keeps the runner clear of the flag until the member is really done; the 10% floor keeps a new runner off the lane's left edge.
- The x position is unchanged in form: `laneX + min(laneW − 22, laneW × place)`.

**Footprints.** The tiles are the lane's history, laid behind the runner:
- the newest tile ends right behind the runner, older ones run leftward at a 10 px pitch (8 px tile, 2 px gap);
- only as many as fit between the lane's left edge and the runner are drawn, newest first;
- a merged lane's footprints start behind its back runner (14 px behind the front one);
- tiles are never repeated, and they no longer scroll.

With `paceOf`, the footprints nearly always fit by themselves (8 calls need 80 px and the runner stands at 131 px), so their length reads as the distance run.

**The ground.** While the lane moves (`running`, `scored`), its ground line is a dashed line (6 on, 4 off) that scrolls left with the existing 1 s `.scroll` loop. Its 10 px period makes the loop seamless; a clip path (id `sabai-race-<lane index>`) keeps it inside the lane. A lane at rest keeps today's solid 1 px ground.

**A failed lane (`out`)** keeps its footprints and its runner's last place, the runner grey as today.

## 2. Slim lanes (idea D)

**States.** Today Main is `waiting` both when it does not work and when it works between calls (the model thinking), which happens all the time. Slimming every `waiting` lane would make Main's lane, and the band's height, jump on every call. So:
- `waiting` — Main is not working (agents still run). **Slim.**
- `thinking` (new) — Main is working with no call in flight. **Full**, drawn as `waiting` is drawn today (the idle runner).
- `finished` — an agent that ended well, for its linger minute. **Slim.**
- Every other state (`running`, `tripped`, `scored`, `out`) stays full. A failed agent keeps its full lane for its linger minute so the failure stays visible.

**A slim lane** is 18 px, one line:
- a status mark at the left: `waiting` an open circle in the track ink (`k-stroke`), `finished` the crew's green check (`statusMark(look, 'done', …)`);
- 16 px from the lane's left, the name, then `· step`, then the time at the right edge, in the same faces and sizes as a full lane's top line;
- the name in `m` (waiting) or `c-ok` (finished). No opacity: it would cut the text's contrast below 4.5:1;
- no ground, footprints, runner, glow or flag.

**Height.** `24 + Σ lanes (42 full, 18 slim) + 6`. The band already takes the tallest of Clawd's panel (112 px), the race and its rows, so nothing else changes. The band's height now changes only on real events: an agent ending, Main starting or stopping, a lane's linger running out — never call by call.

Unchanged: the lanes' order, merging, `+N`, the header, the alt text's form (`thinking` appears as a state word).

## 3. Code

**Data.**
- `RaceLog` (`types/index.d.ts`) gains `count: Record<string, number>`: each member's finished calls, not capped like `calls`.
- `model.ts`:
  - `EMPTY_RACE` gets `count: {}`;
  - `raced` adds one to the member's count;
  - `nextTurnRace` keeps the counts of the agents it keeps, as it keeps their calls;
  - `countOf(log, id)` reads a count, 0 for a log stored before the field existed (atoms outlive a reload), as `doingOf` does for `doing`;
  - `paceOf(count)` as above, with its constants beside `RACE_TILES`.
- `agents.ts`:
  - `Lane` gains `count: number`; a merged lane's is the sum of its members';
  - `LaneState` gains `'thinking'`;
  - `raceOf` sets Main's state to `running` with a call in flight, `thinking` working without one, `waiting` not working (a `tripped`/`scored` timing still wins, as today).
- `svg.ts` `racePart`:
  - the runner at `l.progress ?? paceOf(l.count)`;
  - footprints behind the runner, no repeats, no scrolling tiles;
  - the dashed, clipped, scrolling ground while moving;
  - slim lanes for `waiting` and `finished`, and the height from the lanes' sum;
  - `thinking` drawn with the idle runner, as `waiting` is today.
- `register.tsx`: no change; `racing` already folds calls in through `raced`.

## 4. Testing

**`model.test.ts`**
- `raced` counts a member's calls past 16, while `calls` keeps the last 16.
- `nextTurnRace` keeps the counts of kept agents and drops the rest.
- `countOf` reads 0 from a log without `count`.
- `paceOf`: 0 → .1, 1 → .1, 8 → .5, 100 → .85.
- `raceOf`: a lane's `count`, a merged lane's sum; Main working without a call is `thinking`, not working is `waiting`.

**`svg.test.ts`**
- The lane-per-member test: height `24 + 3 × 42 + 18 + 6` (its `finished` lane is slim), and the finished lane no longer draws a green flag.
- Slim lanes draw their mark, name, step and time, and no runner, flag or tile.
- Footprints: as many tiles as calls when they fit (no repeats), never more than fit; the newest ends at or before the runner's x; none starts left of the lane. This replaces "a lane's tiles stop before its flag", which asserted the repeats.
- `class="scroll"` only on lanes that move; clip ids are unique within a race.
- Every text in a slim lane stays inside the box at 300 px (the box check the usage styles use).

**Unchanged and still passing:** the tree and SVG limit tests in `band.test.tsx` (the drawing only gets smaller), the no-two-class-attributes test (now also over a slim lane and the dashed ground), the theme contrast test.
