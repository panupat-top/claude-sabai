# sabai 2.3.0 — Clawd, usage tiles, a leaner race, settings — design

Date: 2026-10-11 · Mod: `sabai` · Status: approved in conversation (preview at `race2/v23.html` in the session scratchpad), awaiting spec review

Builds on 2.2.0 (branch `sabai-2.2`, not yet merged). The owner reviewed 2.2 on the desktop and asked for five changes. Every choice below is the one recommended in conversation and accepted as a whole.

## Goal

- Clawd says what his mood means.
- Clawd does more: he waves, yawns and looks at what matters.
- The usage limits read at a glance, with no forecast.
- The race box shows who is doing what and for how long, without game counters that mean nothing.
- The settings pane is shorter, and its names match what the band shows.

## 1. Clawd's words

The Clawd panel (comfortable band) shows two lines under him: a headline, then why. Both are built from values the band already reads. Each line is at most 16 characters at the panel's 108 px.

| Mood | Headline | Second line | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| idle | `ready` | `your turn` | — |
| working | `working` | the turn's time, `1m 42s` | `turnStartedAt` |
| startled | `blocked` | `guard stopped N` | the session's guard count (the `guard · N` chip) |
| sad | `tests failing` | `N failed`, in the alert ink | `turnLog.test.failed` |
| celebrating | `all green` | `N passed` | `turnLog.test.passed` |
| sweating | `context full` | `N% · compact?` | the context meter |
| sleeping | `napping` | `idle Nm` (`Nh` from 60 minutes) | `lastTurnAt` |

- `MOOD_WORDS` becomes the headline table. A new pure `moodLine(mood, facts)` in `model.ts` returns the second line, or nothing when its fact is missing.
- The band's alt says both lines: `Clawd: blocked, guard stopped 1`.
- The terminal keeps its glyph; its words are not changed.

## 2. Clawd's tricks

New sprite modes in `sprites.ts`, drawn by editing the sprite's rows (as `sleep` and `dizzy` already shut the eyes). Each starts on a visible frame and loops in ≤ 1 s.

| Trick | When | Drawn as |
|---|---|---|
| `wave` | for 3 s after a main turn completes, while not working | the right claw raised and lowered, two frames, 1 s loop |
| `yawn` | idle from 2 minutes after the last turn, until sleeping | eyes shut to a line, mouth open, a `z` |
| `look-right` | while the race box shows beside him | each eye one column right |
| `look-up` | idle or working, no race, and any usage meter at warn or alert | each eye one row up |

- **Order.** Moods still come first: startled, sad, celebrating and sweating draw their own mode. A trick only replaces `idle` or `run`. Among tricks, the first that applies wins: wave, then look-right, then look-up, then yawn.
- **The wave's end.** `turn.complete` already stamps `lastTurnAt`. It also schedules a redraw for `WAVE_MS + 100` through `$.clock.after`, as the celebration does.
- **Where.** The tricks apply wherever the band draws Clawd: the hero panel, and his spot in the tasks row, info row or live strip.
- **Setting.** Tricks can be turned off (section 5). With tricks off, Clawd draws as in 2.2.
- A pure `trickOf({ mood, now, lastTurnAt, isWorking, raceShown, limitHigh })` in `model.ts` returns the trick or nothing.

## 3. Usage tiles, no forecast

**Forecast removed** everywhere:
- the ghost bar and `→ ~45%` in every layout;
- the terminal's `→`;
- `forecastOf`, `RECENT_MS`, the `usageSamples` atom and `sample()`;
- the `forecast` and `forecastLevel` fields of `Meter`, and the `samples` option of `meters`;
- their tests, and the README's lines about them.

**Tiles** replace `usageRows` in the comfortable band's middle:
- One tile per shown limit, side by side, sharing the middle's width with 8 px gaps. Each tile is 50 px tall on the card colour (`.p`), so its words take the skin's ink (`t`, `m`, `c-*`).
- **Top line:** the label at the left (`CONTEXT`, `5H`, `7D`; mono 9.5, bold, letter-spaced, `m`), and the percent at the right (mono 16, heavy, coloured by level: `c-alert`, `c-warn`, else `c-<kind>`).
- **Bar:** 5 px tall across the tile, less 12 px padding each side, in the chosen bar style, coloured by `look.bar(kind, level)`.
- **Bottom line:** the amount (`743.5k / 1M`) or the reset countdown (`↻ 19m`), mono 10, `m`.
- The middle stacks: info row, tasks row (when present), then the tiles.
- **Narrow tiles.** Under 110 px a tile drops its bottom line. The narrowest case, 760 px with the race, still leaves about 107 px per tile.

Compact density and the stacked band (< 480 px) keep their cards, without the ghost.

## 4. The race box

**Removed:** `LV`, coins, hearts, combo, the far hills, dust, speed lines and confetti. `RaceLog` drops `combo`, `coins` and `mainFails`; `Race` drops `lv`, `coins`, `hearts` and `combo`.

**Header** (24 px):
- left: `N running · M done`. Running counts Main while the turn works, plus each running agent. Done counts the agents ending in the linger minute, and hides at 0.
- right: `✗ n` (failed calls this turn, alert ink, hidden at 0), `✓ n` (calls that succeeded this turn, ok ink), then the elapsed time.
- `RaceLog` keeps `total` and gains `fails`. `turn.start` resets both.

**A lane** is 42 px:
- **Line 1, at the top:**
  - the name, bold, sans 10.5: `Main`, or the agent's **description** (cut to fit), or its type name when it has no description;
  - a merge badge `×N` when merged;
  - `· step` in muted ink: the step in flight, the last result, `Done` or `Failed`, cut to fit;
  - the lane's time at the right. For Main, the turn's time while it works, and none while waiting. For an agent, `now − startedAt`, or `endedAt − startedAt` once ended.
- **Under it, the lane:** ground, tiles (last 16), the runner with its tool in hand and glow, and the flag. The ground now starts at the box's left padding (x 12), since the name has moved up.
- **The type** shows by the hat and its colour, as before.
- **State marks** sit beside the runner, not above it: `OUCH` and the spike to its right, the rising coin to its right, `✓` to its left, `OUT` to its right.
- **Out** draws with shut eyes (the `sleep` rows), grey.

**Many agents of one type:**
1. When more agents are running than the three agent lanes hold, agents of the same type merge into one lane, the type with the most agents first, until they fit.
2. A merged lane is named after the type (`Explore`) with a `×N` badge. Its runners are drawn three deep, the back two faint (opacity .45 and .7).
3. Its step reads `N running`. Its tiles are the newest member's. Its state is `tripped` if any member tripped, else `running`, and it has no progress (the runner holds the middle).
4. If the lanes still do not fit after merging, the `+N` chip stays.

`raceOf` does the merging and returns lanes with `who`, `many?`, `step`, `timeMs?`, `progress?`, `calls`, `doing?` and `state`. Its signature is unchanged.

**Size.** Height = 24 + 42 × lanes + 6. The tree and SVG limits are re-checked with every row on, 3 lanes plus a merged lane, and the widest band.

## 5. Settings pane

The groups, in order, with what each holds:

| Group | Rows |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | Density (Comfortable / Compact, with one sentence on what each shows) · Rows as pill toggles: Info, Tasks, **Activity** (was Live), **Agents** (was Crew), Usage, Toolbar · a hint: "Activity is the race in comfortable, the live strip in compact. Agents are lanes in the race, or the crew row." |
| **Info** | Chips as pill toggles: Folder, Git user, Branch, Node |
| **Usage** | Limits as pill toggles: Context, 5H, 7D · Amber at 60/70/80% · Red at 85/90/95% |
| **Clawd** | Clawd On/Off · Extras as pill toggles: Accessories, Tricks |
| **Agents** | Step reports On/Off |
| **Conversation** | unchanged rows |
| **Safety** | unchanged (the guard and its rules) |
| **Notifications** | Turn notifications, Limit warnings (was Alerts) |
| **Look** | Progress bar style, then the skins (unchanged) |
| — | **Reset to defaults**: sets prefs, the skin and the bar style back to their defaults |

**Pill toggles:** a row of `Button`s, labelled `● Name` when on and `○ Name` when off. The on ones use the primary variant. The desktop draws a Button from its label alone, so the dot carries the state there.

**Prefs:**
- gains `extras: { accessories: boolean; tricks: boolean }`, both on by default;
- with accessories off, `accessoryOf` is not applied;
- with tricks off, `trickOf` is not applied;
- stored prefs from 2.2 load with the new defaults filled in, like `rows` and `chat` today.

Keys and stored values do not change their names (`rows.live`, `rows.crew`); only the labels change.

## Terminal

- The usage line loses the forecast.
- The settings pane follows the same groups. A pill row is a row of Buttons with the same `●`/`○` labels.
- Nothing else changes.

## Testing

**Unit:**
- `moodLine` for every mood, and with each fact missing;
- `trickOf`: each trick, the order between them, moods outranking tricks, the 3 s wave edge, and the yawn window (2 minutes up to sleeping);
- `raceOf`: description names, fallback to type, merging (the most numerous type first, the `×N` count, the newest member's tiles, a tripped member trips the lane), the `+N` chip left over, lane times for Main and agents, and header counts (running, done, ✓, ✗);
- `raced`: `fails` and `total`, reset on turn start.

**SVG:**
- the Clawd panel's two lines;
- each trick's mode draws (eye columns moved, mouth, raised claw);
- tiles: the percent coloured by level, the bottom line dropped under 110 px, words in the card's ink classes (no `st`/`sm` inside a tile);
- race: no `LV`, coin counter, hearts or combo; the header text; the line-1 layout; marks beside the runner; a merged lane's badge and three runners; no element with two class attributes.

**Hooks:**
- a turn's end waves for 3 s and redraws after;
- tricks off and accessories off are honoured;
- reset restores prefs, skin and bar;
- 2.2 prefs load with `extras` filled in.

**Removal:**
- no `forecast` in any band's alt or terminal text;
- `usageSamples` is gone from the contract.

**Limits:** every row on, 3 lanes plus a merged lane, a hat, a trick, the widest band (bodyColumns 496) in all three bar styles: tree < 100,000 characters and SVG ≤ 131,072.

**Contrast:** any new text colour passes 4.5:1 on its own background (`tests/themes.test.ts`, `tests/svg.test.ts`).
